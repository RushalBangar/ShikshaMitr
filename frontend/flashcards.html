Algebraic Identities & Roots Medium Difficulty
Prompt #10.8

What is √144, and what are the roots of x² − 144 = 0?

Hint: Recall integer squares: 10² = 100, 11² = 121, 12² = ... and remember quadratics can yield both positive and negative real solutions.
✓ Answer: ±12
Step-by-Step Proof

The principal square root is positive: √144 = 12 (since 12 × 12 = 144).

For the equation: x² − 144 = 0 ⇒ x² = 144 ⇒ x = ±√144. Therefore, the two distinct roots are x = +12 and x = −12.

🇮🇳 मराठी स्पष्टीकरण (Vernacular Key):

144 चे मुख्य वर्गमूळ 12 आहे. वर्गसमीकरण x² − 144 = 0 सोडवताना x ची दोन मुळे मिळतात: x = +12 आणि x = −12.
